Strategic use of AI

How we use AI

AI helps us answer faster, build better and decide with data — with people in charge of every decision.

RUMi, our AI assistant

Open it from the button at the bottom right of any page.

  • Answers questions in English and Spanish from this site, our Chapter Plan and a curated FAQ — and says so when it doesn’t know.
  • Sends each visitor to the right next step: join, RSVP, sponsor, or hire ELEVATE.
  • Offers free marketing tools for small businesses: a website check, color palettes, QR codes, name ideas and a bio review.
  • Translates the whole conversation when you switch languages.

Privacy and safety

  • Emails and phone numbers are removed before any question reaches an AI model.
  • We use free AI services whose terms say prompts are not used to train their models, plus Chrome’s on-device model when it is already installed.
  • Form submissions never go to an AI. RUMi is always labelled as AI and can make mistakes.

How AI helps us build the site

  • Audits: AI ran performance, accessibility and SEO checks on every page and compared our site with past competition winners.
  • Quality: AI wrote our automated tests — hundreds of layout, zoom, language and phone checks that run before every update.
  • Content: AI drafted English and Spanish versions of recaps and case studies from our team’s notes and Instagram posts.
  • Analytics: AI helped read Google Analytics 4 and turned it into the KPIs on our Impact page.

Decisions we made with AI

AI suggests; our members decide. Every change is reviewed by the Communications team before it goes live.

What AI found

An AI audit found our analytics used a placeholder ID, so no data was being collected.

What we decided

We connected Google Analytics 4 for real and defined key events for each goal.

What AI found

An AI review of winning chapter sites showed they publish their plans and post-event updates.

What we decided

We published our 2026–27 Chapter Plan and a dated recap after major events.

What AI found

AI found a newsletter sign-up that saved nothing — and we don’t run a newsletter.

What we decided

We removed it, and later connected real forms for applications, contact and RSVPs.

What AI found

AI performance tests showed the chat assistant slowed down page loads on phones.

What we decided

RUMi now loads after the page, and inner pages appear about 0.3 s sooner on phones.

What AI found

AI tests of RUMi found gaps: it couldn’t answer about internships or ELEVATE’s real clients.

What we decided

We added those answers, and RUMi now logs questions it can’t answer so we can fill gaps every month.

What it costs

$0. Everything runs on free tiers, so the next board inherits it with no bills.
