ELEVATE Consulting Agency

Student-led. Client-proven.

ELEVATE is our marketing consulting agency. Member teams deliver strategy, branding and campaigns for real businesses and nonprofits in Puerto Rico — supervised by faculty and professionals.

What we deliver

Scoped, supervised and free or low-cost for local businesses and nonprofits.

🧭

Marketing consultation

A one-on-one session reviewing your business, customers and goals — what's working, what isn't, and what you're missing.

🔍

Marketing research

Surveys, interviews, market trends and competitor analysis.

🎨

Content creation

Engaging, consistent content aligned to your goals, from images to copy.

💬

Brand messaging

A review of your brand's voice, tone and content — from your slogan to your social posts.

📊

Google Analytics

Set up and read the data on where your visitors come from and what they do on your site.

🚀

Marketing strategy

Define your ideal customers, the best channels to reach them, and a consistent message.

🖥️

Website assistance

Guidance on keeping your website clear, professional and on-brand.

How we work

One semester, one client, one team of four to six consultants.

Discovery

Kickoff meeting, goals, audience and scope agreement.

Research

Market, competitors and customer insights.

Strategy & creative

Positioning, plan and deliverables, reviewed by advisors.

Launch & handoff

Final presentation, files and a 30-day follow-up.
