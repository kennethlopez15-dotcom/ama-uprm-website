Caribbean Cancer Care Services: «Atrévete a Contestar»

An interactive awareness activation at the 5K/10K Fuerza y Esperanza in Aguadilla, Puerto Rico.

5K/10KFuerza y Esperanza · Aguadilla
5Deliverables

Project context

ELEVATE collaborated with Caribbean Cancer Care Services on a cancer-awareness initiative aimed at encouraging community participation and sparking conversations about the importance of education and prevention. As part of this collaboration, the team took part in the 5K/10K Fuerza y Esperanza, held in Aguadilla, Puerto Rico.

What we did

The team developed the creative concept «Atrévete a Contestar» (“Dare to Answer”), an interactive marketing initiative designed to connect with attendees through questions, interviews and participation activities. During the event, the team captured audiovisual content and engaged with the public to communicate awareness messages in an accessible, participatory way.

Deliverables

Concept development for the «Atrévete a Contestar» campaign.

02

Planning of audience interaction activities.

An event recap video and an interview video.
