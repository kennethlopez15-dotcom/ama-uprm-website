Client project · Digital strategy & UX/UI

Teacher Preparation Program, UPR Mayagüez

Research and a website redesign proposal to make academic information easier to find and strengthen communication with future students.

5Deliverables
3Analyses: market, competitors, digital presence

Project context

ELEVATE partnered with the Teacher Preparation Program (PPM) at the University of Puerto Rico at Mayagüez to identify opportunities to improve its positioning and digital presence. The project aimed to make academic information easier to access and to strengthen communication with future students.

What we did

The team carried out market research, a competitive analysis and an evaluation of the program's digital presence. Based on the findings, it developed marketing recommendations and a website redesign proposal focused on improving navigation, organizing content, and presenting the academic offering, requirements and available resources in a more accessible way.
