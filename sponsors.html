2027 Corporate Partnership

Choose how you want to AMPLIFY

Each student costs about $1,600 to send: airfare, lodging, registration, local transport and competition prep. Pick the level that fits your goals.

Get visible

Impact Partner

$1,000

Build visibility while supporting student professional development.

  • Recognition as an AMPLIFY Supporter in the ICC campaign
  • One dedicated sponsor recognition on AMA UPRM social media
  • Recognition on selected chapter digital communications
  • Share internship, job or student-development opportunities with members
  • QR-code placement at one mutually selected chapter activity
  • Inclusion in our annual sponsor recognition
