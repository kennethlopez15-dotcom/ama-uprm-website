Marketing Week 2026: a week to AMPLIFY

Talks, an ELEVATE workshop for other associations, community service and a fAMAly Friday. Written by the Communications team.

From October 19 to 25, we turned AMPLIFY, this year's chapter theme, into a full week of activities. The goal in our Chapter Plan was simple: show students from every major how marketing can amplify their ideas, their personal brand and their organizations.

Talks with people doing the work

On Tuesday, Yzzy Vicente from MESH Socials and Rosa Rosario from RB Media Group joined us for Behind the Brand, a talk about what a brand needs before it goes digital. On Friday morning, Brandaliz Roldán went online with us for The Brand Behind the Creator, about the strategy behind content creators and what it takes to build a brand of your own.

ELEVATE trained other associations

On Wednesday, our consulting team led From Posts to Impact, a workshop open to every student association at RUM on building a digital presence that works. It is the same work ELEVATE does for its clients, shared with the campus. The dress code was pink for Breast Cancer Awareness Month.

Marketing with a cause

On Thursday, Villa Michelle brought some of their pets looking for a home to the ADEM lobby for Paws for a Cause, with information on fostering and volunteering and a donation drive for the shelter. All week, through Sunday, we also collected food for the Fondita of the Respetable Logia Hijos de la Luz in Yauco.

On camera

Our content team took the mic around campus to ask people what marketing is, sat down with two professionals to talk about what working in marketing actually looks like, and explained why so many brands go pink every October. You can watch all of it on @amauprm.

And the fAMAly

We closed the week the way we like to: together. On Friday evening the chapter went to Don Frappé in Añasco for Sip, Race & Escape, an afternoon of friendly competition and time with the fAMAly.

What's next

Marketing Week is one stop on our way to ICC 2027 in New Orleans. See what is coming on the Events page, read our goals in the Chapter Plan, or join the chapter. Any major is welcome.
