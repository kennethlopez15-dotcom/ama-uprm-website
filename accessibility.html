Accessibility

How we make amauprm.org work for everyone — and how to tell us when it doesn’t.

November 6, 2026, with axe-core 4.10 — including the open chat assistant, the RSVP form and the membership form.

Our commitment

We aim for WCAG 2.2 level AA so every student, partner and family member can use this site.

  • Every page in English and Spanish, with the language set for screen readers.
  • Full keyboard navigation, a “skip to content” link and visible focus outlines.
  • Text alternatives for images and labelled buttons, links and form fields.
  • Layouts that hold up at 200% zoom and on phones from 320 px wide, in portrait and landscape.
  • Animations turn off when your device asks for reduced motion.
  • RUMi works with screen readers: new answers are announced, and on phones it behaves as a dialog you can close with the back button, a swipe or Escape.
  • Automated checks run before every update: hundreds of page, width, zoom, language and gesture combinations.

Known limits

  • The embedded Google Calendar and Instagram images come from those services and may not meet the same standard; every event is also listed as text on our events page.
  • Some older PDFs (annual reports) are designed documents; ask us for the content in another format.

Tell us

Found a barrier? Write to ama@uprm.edu and we will fix it or send the information another way.
