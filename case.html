Illustrative case: a coffee shop rebrand

A four-person ELEVATE team took a Mayagüez café from an inconsistent logo to a full identity and launch plan in ten weeks.

+48%Instagram followers
10Weeks
4Student consultants
3Deliverables

The client

A family-owned coffee shop two blocks from campus, open since 2019 and known among students for its cortado — but running on a logo the owner had made herself in PowerPoint and an Instagram account she updated maybe once a month. She came to ELEVATE through a member who was a regular, looking for a brand that matched how good the coffee actually was.

The challenge

In discovery interviews, the owner put it simply: “People try it once and become regulars, but they walk past it first.” The team's audit found an inconsistent logo across the storefront, menu and social media, no defined color palette, and an Instagram account with no content calendar competing against three chains with full-time marketing staff within a five-minute walk.

What we did

01

Identity

Logo, palette, typography, brand guide.

02

Menu & signage

Printed menu, chalkboard system, storefront.

03

Launch plan

Six-week Instagram calendar and two paid campaigns.

Results

Instagram followers grew 48% over the six-week launch window, measured against the pre-project baseline. The new menu and chalkboard system shipped in week 7, and the owner reported the storefront sign as the single most-commented-on change by regular customers. All three deliverables — identity, menu/signage and the launch calendar — were handed off with source files at the week-10 presentation.

“I finally have a logo I'm not embarrassed to put on a cup. The students gave me a whole calendar of what to post and when — I just had to show up and take the photos.” — Owner, ELEVATE coffee shop client

Example team roles: account lead, brand designer, content strategist and analyst.
