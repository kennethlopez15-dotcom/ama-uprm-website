Who we are

About AMA UPRM

A student-led marketing chapter where members don't just learn marketing — they apply it. Since 2018 at the University of Puerto Rico, Mayagüez.

Mission

To develop marketing leaders through real experience, community engagement and professional growth.

Vision

To be a nationally recognized chapter that transforms students into the next generation of business and marketing professionals.

Founded in 2018, AMA UPRM has been named a Top Collegiate Chapter five years in a row and now ranks among the Top 20 collegiate chapters internationally. Members get workshops, certifications, client projects, competitions and leadership roles — in a community built so that everyone contributes.

How we work

Six teams, one chapter. Every member belongs to a team with a clear scope.

Executive Leadership

President, Executive Vice-President and faculty advisors. Strategy, chapter plan and AMA reporting.

Professional Development

Workshops, speaker series, case competitions and ELEVATE.

Membership

Recruitment, BIG & Little and retention.

Communications

Content, design, video, podcast and this website.

Community Service

Outreach, volunteering and social-impact projects in the west.

Finance

Budget, fundraisers, sponsorships and ICC travel.
