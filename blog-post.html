What we brought home from Chicago

Awards, workshops and the moments that changed how we run the chapter. Written by the Communications team.

Every year, a delegation of us flies to Chicago for the AMA International Collegiate Conference. This year we stood on that stage holding the Puerto Rico flag — and came home with a long list of what to do differently.

The awards

AMA UPRM has been named a Top Collegiate Chapter five years running and now ranks among the Top 20 AMA collegiate chapters worldwide — out of more than 350. We're still tracking down the exact category breakdown and placements from this year's ceremony to publish here by name; check the Awards page for what's confirmed so far.

“The chapters that win are the ones that show their work.” — a judge, during the Website Competition feedback session.

Two things we are changing

First, ELEVATE takes on more client teams next semester so more members graduate with a real portfolio — see Work with ELEVATE. Second, we're running a Panel ICC session earlier in the fall so first-time delegates hear directly from past attendees before they apply — see it on the Events calendar.

See you at ICC 2027

Sponsors cover the gap between what members can pay and what the flights and registration cost — if that's you, see the donation page.
