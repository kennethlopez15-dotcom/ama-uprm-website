Campaign proposal · Sports marketing

Atléticos de San Germán: «Defiende la Cuna»

An integrated media campaign proposal for a BSN professional basketball franchise, built on the team’s history and its mascot, the Monstruo Anaranjado.

BSNProfessional basketball · San Germán
20–30%Attendance growth goal (target, not a result)
6Proposed campaign components

Project context

ELEVATE developed an integrated media campaign proposal for the Atléticos de San Germán that combines strategy and creative work. Its goals: raise attendance at home games by 20–30%, boost official merchandise sales, reignite the rivalries with the Indios de Mayagüez, Leones de Ponce and Piratas de Quebradillas, and create an emotional experience that strengthens team pride and local identity.

The concept

«Defiende la Cuna» (“Defend the Cradle”) builds on San Germán as the cradle of Puerto Rican basketball. The campaign presents the Coliseo Arquelio Torres Ramírez as a symbolic home that fans are called to defend, with the Monstruo Anaranjado as the narrative lead across every channel. The strategy connects the team’s history, a sense of belonging and its rivalries with the experience of going to a game.

Scope of work

Campaign strategy and creative direction, brand storytelling and fan connection, audiovisual concepts for TV, YouTube, Instagram Reels and TikTok, social media content strategy, rivalry marketing, game-day experience design, merchandise strategy, and campaign visual identity.

Concept for a 30-second promotional spot for TV and YouTube.

Concepts for short videos for Instagram Reels and TikTok.

Game posters and digital ads

Game-specific posters and digital advertising built around the mascot.

Game-day experiences

Themed activations inside the Coliseo Arquelio Torres Ramírez.

How success would be measured

Proposed indicators: attendance growth, ticket sales, social media reach and engagement, merchandise revenue, and website traffic and audience growth.

Status: a campaign proposal prepared by ELEVATE. The concepts, materials and targets above are proposed; they are not results of an implemented campaign.
