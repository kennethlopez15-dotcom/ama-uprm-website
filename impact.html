Our three website KPIs

One per goal, measured since November 2026 and reported here every month. How we count each one is explained below.

01

New member applications

Goal: Recruit 30+ new members

  • <strong>Why:</strong> The clearest proof the site grows the chapter.
  • <strong>How:</strong> We count completed applications and how many become paying members.
  • <strong>Link to the goal:</strong> Chapter goal: 30+ new members. We report how many of them applied through the site.
02

Event registrations (RSVPs)

Goal: Professional growth

  • <strong>Why:</strong> They show whether the site fills our events.
  • <strong>How:</strong> We count confirmed RSVPs and compare them with attendance at each event.
  • <strong>Link to the goal:</strong> Chapter goal: 80+ at the February symposium. We report how many attendees RSVP’d through the site.
03

Sponsor and ELEVATE inquiries

Goal: Take 25 students to ICC 2027 and grow ELEVATE

  • <strong>Why:</strong> They tie the site to funding and new clients.
  • <strong>How:</strong> We count inquiries received from the site and how many become sponsors or clients.
  • <strong>Link to the goal:</strong> Chapter goal: $40,000 for ICC and new ELEVATE clients. We report how many partners and clients first contacted us through the site.
How we count each KPI
  • Applications: “Join” clicks (Google Analytics 4 key event), application opens (GA4 event) and completed applications (Google Form responses). Only completed responses count as applications.
  • RSVPs: counted only when our form service accepts the RSVP (GA4 key event), cross-checked with our RSVP log.
  • Inquiries: package and inquiry clicks are GA4 key events (they open an email draft or the ELEVATE Google Form); received emails and form responses confirm each inquiry.
  • Our own automated test visits are excluded from Google Analytics since November 9, 2026, and no personal data is sent to Analytics.

Early baseline

Oct 8 – Nov 5, 2026 (Google Analytics 4). Clicks show interest, not completed applications, and this period includes some of our own test visits, so it is indicative.

≈6%of visitors clicked “Join” in our first four weeks of tracking

What the numbers changed

  • Seven in ten visitors who find us on Google engage with the site, so we invest in search: specific page descriptions, structured data and dated event recaps.
  • We rank first on Google for “AMA UPRM”, but few searchers clicked, so we rewrote our search description to lead with joining, ICC 2027 and dues.
  • Our own automated site checks were being counted as visitors, so since November 9 they are excluded and only real visits are reported.
  • Applications now arrive in Google Forms and RSVPs through the site, so the Membership, ELEVATE and Events teams follow up from shared lists instead of scattered emails.
