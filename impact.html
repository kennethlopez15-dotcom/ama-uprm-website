Our three website KPIs

One per goal, tracked with Google Analytics 4 key events, Google Form responses and our RSVP log since November 2026. Reported here every month.

01

New member applications

Goal: Recruit 30+ new members

  • <strong>Why:</strong> The clearest proof the site grows the chapter.
  • <strong>How:</strong> Three steps, counted separately: “Join” clicks (GA4 key event), application opens (GA4 event) and completed applications (Google Form responses). Only the last one counts as an application.
  • <strong>Target:</strong> 30+ new members
02

Event registrations (RSVPs)

Goal: Professional growth

  • <strong>Why:</strong> They show whether the site fills our events.
  • <strong>How:</strong> Confirmed RSVPs: counted only when our form service accepts the RSVP (GA4 key event), cross-checked with the RSVP log.
  • <strong>Target:</strong> 80+ at the February symposium
03

Sponsor and ELEVATE inquiries

Goal: Take 25 students to ICC 2027 and grow ELEVATE

  • <strong>Why:</strong> They tie the site to funding and new clients.
  • <strong>How:</strong> Package clicks and inquiry clicks (they open an email draft or the ELEVATE Google Form) are GA4 key events; received emails and form responses confirm the inquiry.
  • <strong>Target:</strong> $40,000 and new clients signed

What the numbers changed

  • Seven in ten visitors who find us on Google engage with the site, so we invest in search: specific page descriptions, structured data and dated event recaps.
  • We rank first on Google for “AMA UPRM”, but few searchers clicked, so we rewrote our search description to lead with joining, ICC 2027 and dues.
  • Our own automated site checks were being counted as visitors, so since November 9 they are excluded and only real visits are reported.
  • Applications now arrive in Google Forms and RSVPs through the site, so the Membership, ELEVATE and Events teams follow up from shared lists instead of scattered emails.
