What the numbers changed

  • Google-search visitors engage 4× longer than direct visitors (1:04 vs 0:15), so we invest in search: specific page descriptions, structured data and dated event recaps.
  • People who open RUMi use it about four times each, so every answer now ends with the matching action: join, RSVP, sponsor or hire ELEVATE.
  • Applications and RSVPs now arrive through the site, so the Membership and Events teams follow up from one list instead of scattered emails.
